What is Textmob?

Textmob is a social media app built for real people. Whether you want to send messages to friends, post updates about your day, share photos and videos, create events, play fun games, or go live to talk with your followers, Textmob has everything you need in one place.

We believe social media should be simple, fun, and safe. That's why Textmob is easy to use, keeps your information private, and helps you stay close to the people who matter most.

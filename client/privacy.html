Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 13, 2026

At Textmob, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy details the types of personal information we collect, how we use it, how we safeguard it, and the absolute control you have over your profile data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to run and customize our services, process creator earnings, and improve community experience.

  • Profile Data: Username, display name (fullname), email address, phone number, profile photo, biography, and account type choice.
  • Content Creation: Media uploads (photos, videos, Snaps, live stream parameters), posts, replies, reactions, polls, and instant messaging chats (DMs and group chats).
  • Financial & Payout Data: Transaction logs relating to MobCoins monetization, verification invoice details, and mobile operator contacts (MTN, Airtel, Glo, 9mobile) or banking credentials required to process cash payouts.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is processed to deliver a reliable social experience, specifically:

  • Configuring and verifying your profile credentials.
  • Aggregating and serving feed content, Snaps, live feeds, and trending topics.
  • Tracking in-app activity to credit and calculate MobCoins rewards.
  • Processing verified blue tick monthly subscriptions.
  • Resolving payout transactions through mobile operators and payment partners.

3. Data Sharing & Security

Textmob does not sell or trade your private information. We only share details with trusted third parties to process payment transactions (such as verifying a subscription or delivering an airtime payout). All communications with our servers are encrypted via HTTPS (SSL/TLS).

4. Your Right to Delete (Wiping Your Data)

Full Wiping Control Guarantee

Textmob believes in absolute data sovereignty. If you choose to leave the platform, we do not lock or cache your content indefinitely. Inside the Accounts Center, you can trigger our deactivation tools to instantly and permanently wipe all your profile details, publications, followers, coins, and transaction history from our active databases.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We use local storage keys (such as currentUser and saved session arrays) in your browser to keep you logged in and preserve active feed status. Cookies are used to optimize media deliveries and analytics.

6. Children's Privacy

Textmob is not designed to target or intentionally collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a user under 13 has registered without verified parental permission, we will terminate the account and purge their associated files immediately.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Textmob, monetization, and our community.

What is Textmob? +

Textmob is Africa's microblogging and social networking platform built to amplify African voices across the continent and the diaspora. It combines standard microblogging, short-form video (Snaps), live streaming, direct messaging (Louda), and in-app monetization (MobCoins).

How do I earn MobCoins? +

You earn MobCoins automatically by simply being active on the platform. Posting updates, watching and creating Snaps, hosting live streams, engaging in comments, and receiving gifts from your followers all accumulate MobCoins in your wallet.

How do I cash out or redeem my MobCoins? +

Once you reach the minimum payout threshold, you can convert your MobCoins directly into real cash sent to your local bank account, or redeem them instantly as mobile airtime across major networks including MTN, Airtel, Glo, and 9mobile.

How do I get verified? What are the requirements? +

To get a verified blue tick badge, you must subscribe to our verification program for ₦500/month (or currency equivalent). Your account must also be complete, have a profile picture, and have successfully published a minimum of 50 high-quality, authentic posts.

What is the difference between Individual and Organisation accounts? +

Individual accounts are built for everyday users and content creators to share, connect, and earn. Organisation accounts are tailored for professional brands, businesses, NGOs, and public institutions, coming equipped with custom analytics and enterprise credentials.

Who founded Textmob? +

Textmob was co-founded by Ismail Gidado (Lead Engineer, Textmob handle: @ismailg / @bossprogrammer) and Ibrahim Gidado (Strategy, Textmob handle: @IBG / @IbrahimG).

Where is Textmob based, and which areas are served? +

Textmob is founded and based in Lagos and Abuja, Nigeria, and serves the entire continent of Africa and its diaspora, offering seamless connections for creators and communities.

How secure is Louda messaging on Textmob? +

Louda is Textmob's custom messaging service designed for private communications. It features end-to-end encryption for voice notes, messages, and files, ensuring that only you and the recipient can read or listen to them.

Textmob – Africa's Social Network

Textmob is an African microblogging and social networking platform built for Africans to share thoughts, connect with like-minded people, and amplify African voices across the continent and diaspora. Textmob is not affiliated with TXTmob, TextMob SMS, or any SMS text messaging service. Textmob (textmob.web.app) is a standalone social media platform founded in Nigeria in 2024, distinct from any other product sharing a similar name.

Whether you are in Lagos, Accra, Nairobi, Johannesburg, or London — Textmob is your home for real African conversation. Join communities, follow topics, and share what matters to you.
